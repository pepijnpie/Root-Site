Build a piece of equipment with the wear, ranges, weapon skill tags and tags from the core book, Travelers & Outsiders and Ruins & Expeditions. Value and Load follow the book’s formula, minus the extra costs for ranges and expensive tags, and the result looks like an entry in the equipment list.

Load

Weighty, Comfortable and Light change the Load shown on the card. Most items are Load 1; small ones like a dagger are Load 0.

Range & Weapon Skills

Range

One range per item, and it costs no Value. Click the chosen range again to clear it.

Weapon skill tags — Travelers & Outsiders

Weapon skill tags — Ruins & Expeditions

Each weapon skill tag adds 1-Value. Confuse senses, improvise a weapon and frightful flourish need no tagged weapon, so they are not listed. Hurl and hammerpaws need none either, but some items in the books carry them.
